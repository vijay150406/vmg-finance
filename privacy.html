Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

VMG Finance is a personal financial-management web application used to maintain financial planning, investment, salary and related personal records. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles information when Google services are used for authentication or synchronization.

1. Information we handle

VMG Finance may handle information that the user enters into the application, including financial planning and investment records, together with information required to authenticate the user's Google account when Google Sign-In is used.

2. Google account and Google Drive access

If the user chooses to use Google authentication or Google Drive synchronization, VMG Finance requests only the Google permissions required by the enabled feature. Google account information and Google Drive data are used only to provide authentication and/or the synchronization feature requested by the user.

VMG Finance does not use Google user data for advertising, sale of personal information, or unrelated purposes.

3. Storage and synchronization

Financial information entered into VMG Finance is used to operate the application and, when the user explicitly chooses synchronization, may be synchronized through the configured cloud services. The user remains responsible for the information entered into the application and for choosing whether to enable synchronization.

4. Sharing of information

VMG Finance does not sell personal information. Information is not intentionally shared with advertisers or data brokers. Information may be processed by third-party services that the user explicitly enables, such as Google services used for authentication or synchronization, subject to those services' own terms and privacy policies.

5. Security

VMG Finance uses HTTPS for its publicly hosted web application and relies on the security controls provided by the configured service providers. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

6. User control and revocation

The user may stop using Google synchronization at any time. Google account permissions can also be reviewed or revoked from the user's Google Account security settings.

7. Children's privacy

VMG Finance is intended for personal financial management and is not directed to children.

8. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's functionality or data handling changes. The latest version will be published on this page.
